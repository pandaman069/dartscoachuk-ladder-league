# Closed live-test checklist

Use fictional or test-only personal details for the first trial.

1. Organiser signs in and creates a scheduled season.
2. Two players register on separate devices and confirm their email addresses.
3. Both accounts appear in the organiser player list after refresh.
4. Organiser adds £20.00 test credit to each player with a reason.
5. Players register for the scheduled season.
6. Test the automatic postponement before reaching the minimum player count.
7. Add enough testers, run the scheduled check and verify divisions and ladder
   positions appear on every device.
8. Issue, accept, refuse and cancel normal challenges.
9. Confirm a refused and cancelled Power Play is restored.
10. Arrange an approved venue and match time.
11. Score a best-of-seven match from the participating accounts.
12. Confirm the result from both accounts and verify the ladder movement.
13. Check that notifications and scores update on another device.
14. Confirm a player cannot see organiser controls or change protected data.
15. Delete one disposable test player and verify the account can no longer log
    in.
